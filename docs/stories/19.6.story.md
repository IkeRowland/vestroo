## Story

- As **a traveler requesting a shuttle**, I understand I **must** pick a vehicle class before continuing, with copy that does not sound optional (**FE.19.6**).
- As **a traveler**, I see **image, class, capacity, and luggage** on each card, with **no** price or quote pressure (**FE.19.6**, **FE.10.3**).
- As **a traveler**, I cannot open **passenger details (slide 3)** until I have made a **valid** vehicle choice; I can **change** my mind by selecting another card (**FE.19.6**).

## Acceptance Criteria (ACs)

1. **Required copy:** Visible **header** (and optional **sub-copy** under it or directly under the slide **`<h2>`**) states that choosing a vehicle is **required**; tone matches epic (e.g. title **“Choose your vehicle”** is acceptable if not framed as optional; add **sub-copy** if product wants quote-time reassurance — **epic** lines **129–130**).

2. **Cards:** Each option shows **image** (or placeholder), **class** (`classification` / product-facing label), **passenger capacity**, and **bags/luggage** (`luggageCapacityLabel` or equivalent) — **epic** line **131**; align labels with design if “bags” wording is preferred over “Luggage:”.

3. **No price:** No monetary amounts, estimates, or “from R…” on **slide 2** cards or surrounding copy — **epic** line **131**, **FE.10.3**.

4. **Next gate:** User **cannot** reach **`funnelStep === 2`** (passenger / “slide 3”) without a selected vehicle that exists in the current offer list — **`goNextFromVehicle`** remains authoritative (**epic** lines **132–133**, **139**).

5. **Disabled and/or inline error:** **Next** is **`disabled`** when no vehicle is selected **or** an **inline** error appears on continue attempt per product choice — epic allows **either** pattern (**epic** line **132**); if **Next** stays disabled, ensure **screen-reader** users get equivalent guidance (**NFR.19.2**).

6. **Selection UX:** Tapping a card sets **`selectedVehicleId`**; selecting another card **moves** selection (clear selected styling on prior card) — **epic** line **140**.

7. **Submit:** Slide 3 **Submit** still uses **`validatedSlide2Vehicle`** / **`tripRequestSubmitPayloadSchema`** so a **valid** vehicle is in the payload — **NFR.19.6** / **epic** line **133**.

8. **Tests:** Extend **Vitest** / RTL for required copy (or snapshots), **cannot advance** without selection, **toggle** selection, and **no price** string on slide 2; **`npm run test`** green for affected suites.
