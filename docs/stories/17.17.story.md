## Story



- As **ops staff**, I need **`/ops/comms`**, **`/ops/compliance`**, **`/ops/close-protection`**, and **`/ops/experiences`** to share the same **ops chrome** (headers, filters, tables, row hover, status/avatar primitives) so I can **scan and act** consistently across compliance, close protection, comms templates, and experience bookings.



- As **the codebase**, we need those four routes to **compose** **`ops-primitives`**, **`OpsTableShell`**, **`OpsStatusPill`**, **`OpsAvatarCell`**, and **token**-driven polish **without** changing **RLS**, authorization, or domain logic (**NFR.17.6**) — **presentation and copy structure** only unless **Task 0** records a justified defect.
