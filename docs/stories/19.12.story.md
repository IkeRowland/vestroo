## Story

- As a **traveller**, after I submit a trip request, I need a **clear confirmation** with my **booking reference** and **what happens next**, so I know the request is real and what to expect.
- As a **business booker**, when my request is tied to an organisation, I need a **brief note** that admins can see the trip on the portal, so internal visibility feels transparent.
- As a **keyboard / screen-reader user**, I need **polite live region** feedback and **focus on the headline**, so I land on the success state predictably (**NFR.19.2**).

## Acceptance Criteria (ACs)

1. **Headline:** Slide **4** **`h2`** remains **“Request received”** (**`SLIDE_TITLES[3]`** — ~**66**, ~**665**); no alternate marketing title without PO sign-off.
2. **Booking reference:** **`bookingRefSuccess`** (set from **`result.bookingReference`** on submit success — ~**537**) is **visually prominent** (e.g. dedicated row, **mono** or strong weight). If product approves **copy-to-clipboard**, add an accessible control with success feedback; otherwise ensure **select-all-friendly** presentation (no `user-select-none` on the ref).
3. **“What happens next”:** Exactly **three** concise steps: (**a**) team **reviews** the request; (**b**) **quote by email**, **~30 minutes during business hours** (or agreed copy); (**c**) **payment link** sent **after** you **accept** the quote — **no** PayFast / **“pay now”** on this screen (**[`docs/epic-10.md`](../epic-10.md)** **FE.10.5**).
4. **CTAs:** **“Submit another request”** when **`onExit`** is provided — **same** callback as today (~**704–707**). **“Back to home”** (or PO-approved label) for **standalone** funnel exit to the agreed marketing route — **Task 0** confirms visibility rules for **embedded vs standalone**.
5. **Org note:** When the flow is **linked to an organisation** (same inference as inline org / account context on slide 3), show a **short** sentence that **account admins** can see the trip on the **portal** — no email addresses or tenant ids in UI copy.
6. **Accessibility:** Confirmation content stays inside (or refines) a single **`aria-live="polite"`** region with **`role="status"`** (~**696–697**); **focus** moves to **`#trip-request-slide-heading`** after success (**existing `useEffect`** on **`submitState`** — ~**406–410**). Do **not** regress **FE.19.9** PO error-path focus behaviour on slide 3.
7. **Tone:** Warm, concise, **no** false urgency; **no** instant pricing language (**Epic 10** alignment).
