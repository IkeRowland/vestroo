## Story

- The epic user line frames **US-D1** as **“As ops staff”** because the **outcome** is reliable **`client_type`** on submitted bookings and trustworthy **`/ops/bookings`** **client_type** filters — **implementation** of the Q6 ACs is on the **public / customer booking flow** (email → prompt → submit), not on an ops-only screen.
- As a **booker**, when I enter an email whose domain matches **active** account domains, I see a clear **suggest-and-confirm** prompt with explicit choices so I am not silently booked as an account client; my choice is recorded for ops and audit.
- As **ops staff** (outcome), I need **`client_type`** and **`booking_metadata.client_type_source`** to reflect that explicit choice so **queue filters and downstream account logic** are trustworthy.

## Acceptance Criteria (ACs)

### US-D1 — Client typing at booking form (Theme D, **Q6**)

1. **Q6 prompt copy and two choices:** When the booker’s email domain matches **`authorized_email_domains`** for at least one **active** **`customer_accounts`** row, the booking form shows a confirmation prompt with copy aligned to the epic: *"Looks like you might be booking for **{Account}**. Is this a business booking on their account?"* with two explicit choices: **Yes, use {Account} account** / **No, personal booking** (labels may be shortened for UI if intent is unchanged — document in PR).

2. **Multi-account domain match:** If the domain matches **more than one** active account, show a **select list** of account names (or equivalent single control) to pick the account **before** or **as part of** confirm — **not** only the two-button pattern that assumes a single account.

3. **Dismiss behaviour:** Dismissing the prompt (**click-outside** / **Esc**) sets the effective outcome to **`client_type='walk_in'`** (and no **`customer_account_id`**) — **never** silent **`account_client`**.

4. **`booking_metadata.client_type_source`:** Persist on the booking payload so it is stored in **`booking_metadata`** (shape per **12.1** migration) with value ∈ **`'user_confirmed_domain_match'`** | **`'user_declined_domain_match'`** | **`'no_match'`** | **`'ops_manual'`** — the latter reserved for **12.6** (identify-client); this story only **documents** the enum value, no **12.6** writer.

5. **Documentation:** Inference rules (when each **`client_type_source`** applies, alignment with **`client_type`** / **`customer_account_id`** / **`account_snapshot`**, and relation to DB defaults after **12.1**) are documented in **`docs/data-models.md`** **or** a short **`docs/epic-12-*.md`** addendum **linked from** **`docs/data-models.md`**.
