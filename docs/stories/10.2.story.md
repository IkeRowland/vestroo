## Story

- As a **traveler** completing **Slide 1** of the public **trip request** flow
- I want to enter **pickup** and **drop-off**, **date and time**, **passenger count**, optional **special instructions**, and — **when pickup is an airport** — my **flight number**, with **clear validation** and **accessible** inputs on **mobile**
- so that **operations** receive accurate ride context **without** seeing **pricing** or **payment** in the funnel, and the experience stays **one page** with **progressive disclosure** for airport pickup (**[`docs/epic-10.md`](../epic-10.md)** **FE.10.2**, **Design Goals**)

## Acceptance Criteria (ACs)

### Field coverage — normative (FE.10.2)

1. **Pickup address (required):** Slide 1 **MUST** include a **pickup address** (or equivalent **service-point** capture aligned with product naming) control. The traveler **MUST NOT** be able to treat pickup as empty when advancing per **Slide 1 validation ACs** — **[FE.10.2](../epic-10.md)**.

2. **Drop-off (required):** Slide 1 **MUST** include **drop-off** **service point / address** (wording **MAY** match product copy). It **MUST** be **required** with validation consistent with **AC12** — **[FE.10.2](../epic-10.md)**.

3. **Date and time (required):** Slide 1 **MUST** capture **date and time** of the ride (single control or paired controls — **implementation detail**). Both **date** and **time** **MUST** be **required** — **[FE.10.2](../epic-10.md)**.

4. **Number of passengers (required):** Slide 1 **MUST** capture **number of passengers** as a **required** field with **sensible** constraints (for example, **minimum 1**, **upper bound** per product/data rules if defined — otherwise document default in PR notes) — **[FE.10.2](../epic-10.md)**.

5. **Special instructions (optional):** Slide 1 **MUST** include **special instructions** as an **optional** field (user **MAY** leave it blank; validation **MUST NOT** block progression solely for omission) — **[FE.10.2](../epic-10.md)**.

### Airport pickup — detection UX and conditional Flight Number

6. **Product rules for “airport pickup” (documented):** The implementation **MUST** follow **product rules** for when pickup is treated as an **airport** (for example, **address autocomplete** resolving to an airport, **manual “This is an airport pickup”** toggle, **or both**). If the **exact algorithm** is **TBD**, this story **MUST** still ship **conditional** Flight Number behaviour per **AC7–AC9**, and the **PR or Dev Technical Guidance** **MUST** record the **chosen UX** and any **placeholder** heuristic so a future story can tighten detection without breaking **FE.10.2** semantics.

7. **Flight Number visible when airport applies:** When pickup **is** identified as an **airport** per **AC6**, the UI **MUST** show a **Flight Number** field — **[FE.10.2](../epic-10.md)**.

8. **Flight Number required when visible:** When **Flight Number** is **shown** (**AC7**), it **MUST** be **required** (user **MUST NOT** advance past Slide 1 valid state without it, unless product explicitly waives in writing — **default: required**) — **[FE.10.2](../epic-10.md)**.

9. **Flight Number hidden when not airport:** When pickup **is not** an airport per **AC6**, the **Flight Number** field **MUST** be **hidden** (not merely disabled), and its value **MUST NOT** be required for validation — **[FE.10.2](../epic-10.md)**.

### Alignment with FE.10.1 (shell)

10. **One page, no step routes:** Delivering Slide 1 fields **MUST NOT** add **distinct application routes** for this step. The traveler **MUST** remain on the **same primary URL** as **Story 10.1** for the whole funnel — **[FE.10.1](../epic-10.md)**; **[`docs/stories/10.1.story.md`](10.1.story.md)**.

11. **Inside the unified form:** Slide 1 controls **MUST** live **inside** the **existing** **FE.10.1** **form section** (same **`<form>`** / unified semantics as implemented in **10.1**), not a separate form or iframe — **[FE.10.1](../epic-10.md)**.

12. **Slide 2–3 unchanged in scope:** **Stories 10.3+** remain **placeholders** on Slides **2–3** unless explicitly merged; **10.2** **MUST NOT** implement **vehicle cards**, **passenger/phone** fields, or **submit persistence** — **Explicit out of scope** above.

### Validation and error handling

13. **Required field validation:** On **Next** from Slide 1 (or equivalent **primary forward** action), **required** fields (**pickup**, **drop-off**, **date/time**, **passengers**, and **flight number** when **AC7–AC8** apply) **MUST** be validated; **clear** error messaging **MUST** identify **which** field(s) failed — **pattern** **SHOULD** match existing app forms (**[`docs/epic-10.md`](../epic-10.md)** intent).

14. **Optional instructions:** **Special instructions** **MUST** remain **non-blocking** when empty — **AC5**.

15. **No pricing validation:** Slide 1 **MUST NOT** compute, display, or validate **rand amounts**, **quotes**, **estimates**, or **totals** — **[Non-Goals](../epic-10.md#non-goals)**.

### Accessibility

16. **Labels and errors:** Every Slide 1 **textual** input, **select**, and **datetime** control **MUST** have an **associated** **visible** and/or **programmatic** **label** (`<label>` **for** / **`aria-labelledby`**) and **error** text **MUST** be **associated** with the control (for example, **`aria-describedby`**, **`aria-invalid`**) — **[Design Goals](../epic-10.md)** (**accessible** progressive disclosure).

17. **Airport / flight disclosure:** When **Flight Number** **appears** or **disappears** (**AC7**, **AC9**), the change **MUST** be **perceivable** to assistive technology (for example, **focus** or **`aria-live`** **polite** announcement of new required field — **consistent** with **FE.10.1** shell step-change patterns).

### Mobile and layout

18. **Mobile-first usability:** Slide 1 **MUST** remain **usable** on **narrow** viewports: inputs **MUST NOT** require horizontal scrolling at typical mobile widths; touch targets **SHOULD** meet project conventions — **[Design Goals](../epic-10.md)** (**Responsiveness**).

### Non-functional guardrails

19. **Performance (NFR.1.1):** Conditional **Flight Number** UI **SHOULD** minimize **CLS** (reserve space or animate **without** shifting unrelated chrome); avoid **new** **heavy** **blocking** scripts for address/autocomplete unless justified — **Constraining NFRs** above.

20. **Security / SEO posture (NFR.3.1, NFR.3.2):** **MUST NOT** weaken **HTTPS** posture for this page. **SHOULD** avoid **new** route-level SEO changes beyond what **10.1** already established — **Constraining NFRs** above.
