## Story

- As an **operations lead or authorised staff user**
- I want **`/ops/*`** to cover **core staff operations capabilities** (users/roles, corporate clients, fleet and categories, shuttle service geography, bookings lifecycle, and grouped related workflows) with **clear** routes, **honest** backlog status, and **aligned** navigation
- so that **day-to-day B2B shuttle operations** are **not blocked** by missing console surfaces, **silent** feature drift, or **orphaned** admin actions — while **chauffeur** and **traveller** experiences stay **properly** separated

## Epic traceability (source)

**From [`docs/epic-5.md`](../epic-5.md) — FE.5.11:** This story is the **canonical checklist** for what **authorised staff** (roles as product defines: e.g. dispatcher, ops admin) MUST be able to do in the **`/ops/*` console**, using patterns from **FE.5.2–FE.5.3**. It **supplements** FE.5.4–FE.5.5 (capstone mapping) by stating **Vestroo outcomes** regardless of reference gaps.

The system MUST support, at minimum as **implemented or explicitly backlog-scoped** with status in the traceability matrix:

1. **Staff users and roles** — Create and maintain **internal** staff identities, **assign roles** or permissions, deactivate access; align with **Supabase Auth** / custom claims and **`requireOpsStaffPage`** (see **[Epic 6](epic-6.md)** **BE.6.5**, **BE.6.7**). **Chauffeur / field** accounts remain distinct from generic “users” where VST separates them.
2. **Corporate clients** — Create and manage **client / account** records (companies, contract metadata as product requires) for **B2B** shuttle context; link to bookings where applicable.
3. **Vehicles and categories** — Create, **categorise** (classes/types), update, and retire **fleet** records; optional **media** per **FE.5.4** (Supabase Storage).
4. **Service routes and service areas** — Create and manage **corporate shuttle** routing concepts: corridors, zones, approved pickup/drop-off, **pricing-relevant** path data—**not** public-transit **fixed-line** or **mass-transit network** IA (see **Epic 9** for deferred capstone-only modules).
5. **Bookings** — Create (where staff-assisted booking is required), search, filter, view detail, and perform **lifecycle** actions (assign, modify, cancel, fulfil) consistent with **Epic 1** / **VST-6** and existing **`/ops/search`**, **`/ops/fulfil`**, **`/ops/trips`** behaviour; gaps MUST be listed in the matrix.
6. **Related staff workflows** — As product requires: **roster / calendar**, **drivers or chauffeurs**, **invoicing hooks**, **compliance**, **experiences**, **vehicles**, **board**—already partially present under **`/ops/*`**; FE.5.11 MUST **not** duplicate every VST story but MUST ensure the **Epic 5 sidebar IA** (FE.5.1) **groups** these domains and **no critical** admin activity is **orphaned** without a mapped route or **explicit deferral**.

**Epic 2** covers **PayloadCMS** and **marketing** content where still in use; **day-to-day operational staff work** for the items above is **owned Epic 5** plus **data/actions in Epic 6**.
