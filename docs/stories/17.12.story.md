## Story

- As **ops staff**, I need **`/ops/vehicles`** to support a **dense list** (default) **or** a **scannable card grid** with **URL-stable** **`view`**, and a **detail rail** with **hero**, **status**, **features**, and **reminders**-aligned content (**Wheelzie image 8**), so I can **inspect fleet state** and **act** (open / assign) **without losing context** on refresh or shared links.

- As **the codebase**, we need a **new `OpsCardGrid`** primitive (**FE.17.6**) so future **`/ops/*`** surfaces (e.g. experiences) can **reuse** the same **card chrome**, aspect ratio, and **status** patterns — alongside **17.9** split layout for **FE.17.5** adoption on this route.
