## Story

- As a **product, compliance, and engineering** stakeholder with a recorded **go** at **[`#sh-9-1`](../epic-9.md#sh-9-1)** for a **patterned / capacity-managed** corporate shuttle offering
- I want **Postgres** tables (or disciplined extensions to existing **`public.*`** entities) that model **recurring runs**, **waypoints or pickup order**, and **timetables** as product defines — with **RLS** — using **Vestroo / VST** vocabulary (**run**, **assignment**, **service window**, **waypoint**, **manifest** per **ADR**), and a **resolved** relationship to today’s **service route** and **quote** concepts
- so that **SH.9.3+** can build **inventory-safe** capacity and ops flows **without** vocabulary drift, **without** transit demo branding, and **without** ambiguous overlap between **marketing / booking quotes** and **published run timetables**
