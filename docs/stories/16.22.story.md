## Story

- As **any ops staff member**, I need the **sidebar** to show **walk-in** and **account** **primary** queues **above** the **master** **Bookings** list, with **Fulfil** and **Board** **clearly** marked **legacy**, so my **mental model** matches **Q22** and I still have **Q20** triage in one place.

- As **ops staff**, I need a **Dispatch** entry when Theme C is in flight, with **safe** behaviour if the page is not ready yet.

- As **staff** navigating deep links, I need **breadcrumbs** to show **correct segment labels** for the new and future **ops** paths.

- As **a future finance-first role** (Theme H), I must see **reconciliation** surfaces (**Bookings**, **Invoicing**, **Compliance**) without **primary** **workflow** queues I do not run day-to-day (per **FE.5.1** / epic **US-A3**).
