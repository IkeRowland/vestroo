## Story

- As a **traveler** starting a **trip request** on the public site
- I want a **single booking page** with **three clear steps** (**Ride details** → **Choose your ride** → **Passenger details**) implemented as **in-page slides** inside **one form section**, with **usable** controls on **mobile** and **assistive technology**
- so that I understand the flow, never get sent to **different URLs** for each step, and the experience stays **calm**, **progressive**, and **aligned** with **[FE.10.1](../epic-10.md)** while **full fields** and **submission** are delivered in **follow-up stories**

## Acceptance Criteria (ACs)

### Shell — routing and structure

1. **Single page, no step routes:** The booking funnel **MUST** be reachable as **one** page (one primary route / URL for this flow). The traveler **MUST NOT** use **distinct application routes** to move between **Ride details**, **Choose your ride**, and **Passenger details** — **[FE.10.1](../epic-10.md)**.

2. **One form section:** The three steps **MUST** live inside **one** semantically coherent **form section** (for example, a single `<form>` wrapping all steps, or one `<form>` with **one** submit affordance deferred to a later story, per technical choice) such that the implementation **reads** as a **unified form** rather than three unrelated regions — **[FE.10.1](../epic-10.md)**.

3. **Three slides, client state:** The UI **MUST** implement **exactly three** sequential **slides** (steps). Moving between slides **MUST** use **client-side state** (step index / state machine); **full page reloads** or **route changes** **MUST NOT** be required to change steps — **[FE.10.1](../epic-10.md)**.

4. **Step labels:** Each slide **MUST** expose a clear **visible title** (or **legend**) consistent with the epic’s step intent: **Ride details**, **Choose your ride**, **Add passenger details** (wording **MAY** minor-copy-edit but **MUST** remain unambiguous vs **[FE.10.2–FE.10.4](../epic-10.md)** intent).

### Navigation and placeholders (stub-allowed)

5. **Primary navigation:** The shell **MUST** provide **Next** and **Back** (or equivalent **primary** navigation) between slides **without** leaving the page. **Final** submit behaviour **MAY** be **disabled**, **omitted**, or **stubbed** (non-functional **Submit** label is acceptable) — **working** trip-request **submission** and **FE.10.5** behaviour are **explicitly out of scope** for this story.

6. **Placeholder surfaces for later stories:** **Slides 2 and 3** **MUST** render **placeholder** or **skeleton** content (for example, short helper text and/or non-interactive placeholders) that **occupies** the slide region and **documents** where **FE.10.3** / **FE.10.4** UI will attach — **without** implementing those stories’ **fields**, **validation**, or **data wiring**.

7. **Slide 1 placeholder policy:** **Slide 1** **MAY** use **minimal** placeholder content **or** a **sparse** stub consistent with **Story 10.2** — **full** **FE.10.2** capture (pickup/drop-off, datetime, passengers, instructions, conditional flight number) is **out of scope** unless **Story 10.2** is explicitly merged into the same PR (default: **defer** to **10.2**).

### Accessibility

8. **Focus and order:** Focus order **MUST** follow a **logical** reading order on each slide. When the active slide **changes**, focus **MUST** move to a **sensible** target (for example, the new slide **heading** or first focusable control) **or** an **`aria-live`** announcement **MUST** inform users of the new step — **no** “silent” step changes for screen-reader users.

9. **Naming:** All **buttons**, **step indicators**, and **form controls** introduced by this shell **MUST** have **accessible names** (visible text and/or **`aria-label`** as appropriate). If a **stepper** or **progress** pattern is used, it **MUST** expose **current step** to assistive tech (for example, **`aria-current="step"`** on the active step or equivalent).

### Mobile and layout

10. **Mobile-first:** Layout **MUST** be **mobile-first**: content **MUST** reflow at small widths; **Next** / **Back** (and any step chrome) **MUST** remain **usable** on a **narrow** viewport — **[Design Goals](../epic-10.md)** (**Responsiveness**).

### Non-functional and product guardrails

11. **No payment or pricing in shell:** The shell **MUST NOT** embed **PayFast**, **card** UI, **rand** amounts, **quotes**, **totals**, or **fare breakdown** — **[Non-Goals](../epic-10.md#non-goals)**; **FE.10.5** submission rules apply to **later** stories (**[`docs/epic-10.md`](../epic-10.md)**).

12. **Performance posture (NFR.1.1):** Implementation **SHOULD** avoid **unnecessary** **CLS** from slide transitions and **SHOULD** avoid adding **heavy** **blocking** scripts to the booking entry’s first paint beyond what the stack already requires — **reasonable** effort; document any known trade-off in PR notes.

13. **Transport and SEO posture (NFR.3.1, NFR.3.2):** **MUST NOT** introduce **insecure** transport for this flow. **SHOULD** follow existing **Next.js** / **ISR** (or **static**) conventions for the **host** route if the booking page **reuses** marketing patterns — align with **[Related Non-Functional Requirements](../epic-10.md#related-non-functional-requirements)**.
